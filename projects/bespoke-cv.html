Bespoke CV

Agentic AI tool that rewrites your CV for every job, surgically not generically

Python Streamlit Gemini 2.5 Flash 0→1

Context

The Problem

⏳
Manual rewriting is exhausting Job seekers spend 20-40 minutes per application tailoring their CV to each role. Most of that time is spent on guesswork about what to change.
🤖
Generic AI tools destroy formatting Existing AI rewriters regenerate the entire CV from scratch, losing carefully crafted structure, bullet hierarchy, and Word formatting that recruiters expect.
🚫
ATS systems reject missing keywords Applicant Tracking Systems silently filter out CVs that don't mirror the exact language of the job description, even when the candidate is qualified.

Product

The Solution

  • Upload your CV and paste a job URL. Bespoke CV extracts and parses the full job description automatically.
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash identifies only the weak or misaligned bullets, leaving strong ones untouched.
  • A side-by-side diff shows every proposed change. You accept or reject each edit individually.
  • A live ATS score and JD match score update in real time as you accept changes.
  • Downloads as a .docx with your original formatting, fonts, and structure fully preserved.

Walkthrough

How It Works

1 Input your CV and job URL

Upload a DOCX and paste the job listing URL. BeautifulSoup extracts and parses the JD; pdfplumber extracts your CV text.

assets/bespoke/inputfilled.png
2 Analysis in progress

Gemini 2.5 Flash reads both documents and identifies which bullets to rewrite, with temperature=0 for deterministic, consistent output every time.

assets/bespoke/analyzing.png
3 Score dashboard

An ATS keyword score and JD match percentage surface immediately. Both update live as you accept or reject suggested edits. The JD match score is weighted by bullet-level impact: each bullet is individually scored for impact (1-10) and the overall score reflects the weighted average, not just keyword presence. ATS keywords from the JD are deliberately woven into the rewritten bullets, so the ATS score improves through the rewrites themselves rather than through a separate keyword-stuffing step.

assets/bespoke/scores.png
4 Diff View: Accept or Reject each change

Review every proposed rewrite in a before/after diff. Accept what fits, reject what doesn't. The ATS and JD match scores update with each decision. Each rewritten bullet includes an explanation of which JD keywords were incorporated and why, so you understand exactly what changed and how it improves your ATS match.

assets/bespoke/diffview.png
5 Download your tailored CV

Export as .docx with your original formatting, fonts, and structure fully intact, ready to submit. No cleanup needed.

Thinking

Key Design Decisions

temperature=0 for consistency. Job applications are high-stakes. Randomness in output undermines trust. Users need to know the same CV + JD pair produces the same suggestions every time, so they can compare runs and share results.
Surgical edits, not full rewrites. The model is explicitly instructed to only touch weak or misaligned bullets, not rewrite the entire CV. This preserves the user's voice and avoids the uncanny-valley problem of AI-sounding language replacing authentic experience.
Word output to preserve formatting. Recruiters receive and screen CVs in Word. Exporting as .docx via python-docx, with layout reconstructed from the original file, means no reformatting work for the user after download.

Product thinking

PM Decisions

Diff view over full rewrite. Users trust surgical edits more than a black-box rewrite. Seeing exactly what changed builds confidence in the output.
User controls every change. AI suggests, human decides. The tool keeps your voice intact by never forcing an edit.
Live score as you tick checkboxes. Real-time feedback changes how you engage with suggestions. You stop guessing and start optimising.
Missing keywords solved inside rewrites. Surfacing a list of missing keywords without fixing them is anxiety, not a product. Bespoke CV solves the problem, not just names it. ATS keywords are woven into rewrites naturally, not appended as a separate list.

Reflection

What I Learned

AI output is only as consistent as your prompt. temperature=0 alone was not enough. The prompt needed to explicitly evaluate every single bullet, not just the obvious weak ones.
Format preservation is not just a trust problem, it is a product completeness problem. If the output looks different from the input, users assume something went wrong. Worse, they have to reformat manually, which defeats the entire purpose of the tool. The CV should be submission-ready on download, no cleanup needed.
The score means nothing without context. A match score alone is vanity. Weighted scoring by bullet impact is what makes it actionable.
Showing the reasoning builds trust more than the output itself. The explanation under each rewritten bullet (why it was changed, which keywords were added) mattered as much as the rewrite. Users don't just want a better bullet. They want to understand why it's better.
Not every job application starts with a URL. Sometimes you have only the JD text, copied from an email, a PDF, or a company portal. Bespoke CV solves that with a manual JD paste option, so the tool works regardless of how you found the role.
